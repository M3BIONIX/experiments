# Food, cost and neural learning experiment

Paired training did not produce a consistent trained-logo first-approach advantage over both controls across all tests. 2 paired probes received target penalties, with 0 returns after a fully delivered penalty. Every recorded first approach was toward the initially faced east position. Addiction is not established.

48 conditions and 192 reward-free behavioral probes; four logos × four target positions × three controls. Guided training starts are not counted as choices.

The environmental loss is movement cost plus target-entry penalty minus virtual food benefit. Positive outcomes drive PAM11, costs drive PPL101, and the existing experimental plasticity rule updates synapses. No loss gradient is backpropagated and there is no guarantee this rule minimizes the loss. The food-to-current and cost-to-current conversions are programmed model assumptions; they do not model metabolism or prove subjective reward/aversion.

## Acquisition and cost exposure

| Control | Central food contacts /16 | Penalty-exposed probes /16 | Returns after a fully delivered penalty | Cue PAM11 spikes | Blank-panel PAM11 spikes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| paired | 3 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| unpaired | 3 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| frozen | 4 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

A return requires leaving at least 4 mm and entering the 2 mm target zone again after an earlier penalty has actually been delivered. A fly that never entered the penalty zone did not demonstrate resistance to punishment. Zero cue spikes do not mean dislike. Native cue readouts had both external current channels switched off.

The three brief near-food episodes supply exposure, followed by a central food-available acquisition episode. Unpaired controls receive the paired arm's positive dose later on white frames. Negative movement outcomes remain contingent on each arm's own movement. Learning-disabled controls retain initial synapses. All four test branches restore trained synaptic memory with common transient dynamics.

Tests last up to three seconds; the extinction branch adds a two-second unrewarded rendered cue exposure. The three-second window and 16 mm observation boundary limit opportunities to leave and return; zero returns do not establish absence of longer-term seeking. This is a short mechanistic pilot, not a validated addiction assay. The neural physiology, sensory projection, motor adapter and PPL101 cost interpretation remain experimental. Even successful persistence would only be a model behavior requiring further validation. Continuing to walk while this loss rises could reflect an ineffective controller or an unvalidated cost mapping, not addiction.

Every outcome, current dose, measured spike rate, body trajectory and training weight archive is retained. See probes.csv for all outcomes and summary.json for dwell, distances, losses, exposure counts and matched controls.
