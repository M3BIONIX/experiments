## Interpretation

In the mixed-logo arena, 3 trials contained at least one PAM11 spike; there were 3 PAM11 spikes in total. These mixed-view spikes cannot identify which logo caused them.

First approach is an operational proximity measure, not a learned preference. PAM11 firing counts are generated by the spiking model; they do not measure dopamine concentration, pleasure, or addiction. Ties and zeros must remain ties and zeros. All four logos are visible in the arena, so per-logo neural comparisons come from separate isolated exposures, not from assigning mixed-view spikes to a nearby logo.

The RGB-to-neuron projection and the connection from neural motor outputs to the FlyGym walking controller are engineering approximations. This is not validated real-fly behaviour. No addiction conclusion is supported, regardless of the count ranking.

## Protocol

- Main experiment: 100 independent seeds, randomized logo locations and initial headings, up to 3 simulated seconds per trial; earlier stop on leaving the observation area or falling.
- Same white-conditioned neural state restored before each trial; experimental plasticity allowed within the trial.
- No automatic video reward or manual current in any logo trial. No baseline forward drive is injected: walking drive comes from MN9/DNp09 firing, steering from DNa02 asymmetry.
- Isolated comparisons: 25 matched size/position/brightness variants per logo plus 25 blank controls, 200 ms per exposure; frozen plasticity and restored initial state.
- A separate artificial-current positive control verifies the PAM11 mechanism and is excluded from all logo results.
- Recorded positions were independently reprocessed to verify first-approach counts and dwell times. Neural/body time agreement and zero artificial stimulus were checked for every trial.

## Scope

The live arena at port 8766 still uses the original contrast baseline. These connectome-driven results are a separate batch experiment. The completed 100-trial contrast batch is stored separately under `app/results/logo-batch-100` and is not used as neural evidence.
